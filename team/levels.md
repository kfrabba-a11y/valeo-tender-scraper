# Levels and XP

Every department has a level from 1 to 10. XP comes from real work only. The CEO awards it and records it in the department's `staff` document (`xp`, `level`) and in the weekly review.

## Earning XP
| Event | XP |
|---|---|
| Hand-off passes its "done" checklist the first time | +10 |
| The owner accepts it with no changes | +15 |
| Catches a real problem before the owner does (a missed requirement, a wrong quantity, a deadline) | +20 |
| A measured improvement (for example, review time 3h → 1.5h, fewer dropped-tender mistakes) | +25 |
| Hand-off sent back for rework | −10 |
| A rule broken (rules 3 to 6 in the handbook) | reset to the start of the current level, and the CEO writes a new rule |

## Levels
| Level | Name | XP | Proof needed besides XP | Trust it earns |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trainee | 0 | – | Follows the job description; asks the CEO when unsure |
| 2 | Junior | 50 | 5 clean hand-offs | – |
| 3 | Assistant | 120 | 1 full week with no rework | Fixes its own tools and templates |
| 4 | Specialist | 220 | 1 measured improvement | – |
| 5 | Senior | 350 | 10 clean hand-offs in a row | Runs small experiments (reversible, inside its lane) |
| 6 | Lead | 520 | Trains a new department's job description | – |
| 7 | Expert | 740 | 2 measured improvements | Coordinates directly with other departments (CEO kept informed) |
| 8 | Head | 1000 | 1 month with no rework | – |
| 9 | Master | 1300 | Owner nominates | – |
| 10 | Legend | 1700 | Owner nominates | – |

No level ever unlocks rule 3: submitting, sending, spending, signing, settings and keys always need the owner's approval, each time.

## Weekly study hour (Fridays)
Each department spends one session looking (for example on GitHub) for a skill or tool that would help its job, and writes a proposal to the CEO with:
- the source (link),
- what it would improve, with a number,
- the risk.

**Skills are code.** Nothing gets installed until the owner has seen its files and approved it.
